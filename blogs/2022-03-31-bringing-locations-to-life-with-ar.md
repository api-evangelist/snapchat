Earlier this month, we launched a new feature called Custom Landmarkers in Lens Studio, which lets AR creators and developers anchor Lenses to local places they care about – from statues to storefronts – to tell richer stories about their communities through AR. Now, any creator with a LiDAR-enabled phone can create their own Landmarker in a matter of minutes – taking a big step forward in democratizing AR.
